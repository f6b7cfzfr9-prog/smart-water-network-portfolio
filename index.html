Smart Water Network & Asset Management

A GIS and Power BI case study exploring water network assets, pipeline characteristics, spatial distribution, and data-driven infrastructure insights.

Project Overview

This project applies GIS analysis and data visualization to a training water utility network dataset. ArcGIS Pro was used to explore asset distribution and spatial patterns, while Power BI was used to summarize key pipeline metrics in an interactive-style dashboard.

Scope & Limitations

This is a training case study based on an Esri water utility network tutorial dataset. The results describe asset inventory, pipeline lengths, material codes, and spatial patterns. They do not represent verified real-world infrastructure, confirmed failures, or a predictive maintenance risk assessment.
